About

Pro Wrestling Researcher is a local browser over a Postgres-backed archive of pro wrestling literature, periodicals, territories, wrestlers, factions, and ranking lists. Records are seeded from curated sources, then enriched against public catalogs (Open Library, Google Books, Library of Congress, the Internet Archive, and Cagematch).

Confidence levels

high   {{ confidence.get('high', 0) }} books
Hand-curated; verified author, year, publisher, and synopsis.
medium   {{ confidence.get('medium', 0) }} books
Single-source attribution from Open Library or Google Books — generally trustworthy.
medium_search   {{ confidence.get('medium_search', 0) }} books
Resolved through Exa neural search or Internet Archive fallback; the match looked plausible but warrants a human glance.
low   {{ confidence.get('low', 0) }} books
Title-only entries — confirmed real books but author / year / ISBN need enrichment.
low_searched   {{ confidence.get('low_searched', 0) }} books
Searched all sources and matched nothing wrestling-related; needs manual review.

Enrichment pipeline

Books with low or medium confidence are run through enrich_metadata.py, which queries Open Library, Google Books, the Library of Congress, the Internet Archive, and (optionally) Exa neural search in turn.

cd bibliography/
python3 enrich_metadata.py --limit 50            # try a small batch first
python3 enrich_metadata.py --reset-failed        # retry low_searched rows
python3 enrich_metadata.py --book-id 123         # work a single book
python3 enrich_metadata.py --google-key KEY      # higher Google Books rate limits
python3 enrich_metadata.py --exa-key KEY         # enable Exa neural fallback
python3 enrich_metadata.py --workers 16          # parallel API requests
python3 enrich_metadata.py --dry-run --verbose   # preview without writing

Faster variants for large batches: enrich_chunk.py (chunked) and enrich_parallel.py (worker pool).

Schema

books(id, title, subtitle, category_code, publisher, year_published,
      isbn10, isbn13, pages, format, language, country, subject_wrestler,
      era, territory_or_promotion, synopsis, source_url, confidence,
      primary_source_value, created_at)
authors(id, name, ring_name, is_wrestler, notes)
book_authors(book_id, author_id, role)          -- author | co-author | as told to | editor | foreword
categories(id, code, label, description)

periodicals(id, title, publisher, country, language, year_started, year_ended,
            frequency, type, parent_company, notes, issue_count_known,
            archive_in_collection, source_url, confidence, created_at)
periodical_issues(...)                          -- individual issues, with covers
issue_cover_subjects(...)                       -- which wrestlers appeared on a cover

territories(id, name, short_name, region, nwa_member, headquarters_city,
            headquarters_state, year_founded, year_closed, promoter_lineage,
            notes, country, aliases, cagematch_id, cagematch_url, created_at)

wrestlers(id, legal_name, primary_ring_name, other_ring_names, born_date,
          died_date, living, debut_year, retired_year, primary_role,
          hometown_billed, hometown_real, finisher, style, socials,
          convention_status, last_known_appearance, footage_notes,
          midcard_files_status, midcard_files_priority, why_they_mattered,
          notes, cagematch_id, cagematch_url, created_at)
wrestler_territory_runs(id, wrestler_id, territory_id, start_year, start_month,
                        end_year, end_month, role_during_run,
                        ring_name_during_run, primary_run, notes)

factions(...)         faction_members(...)
ranking_lists(...)    ranking_entries(...)      -- e.g., PWI 500 historical rankings
research_sources(id, url, description, consulted_at, notes)
